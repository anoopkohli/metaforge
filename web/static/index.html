Many companies, one app, and AI that cannot mix them up

metaforge is a pretend business app shared by five made-up companies (tenants). Each one has its own records and documents. Hidden inside every company's data is a unique secret marker, a canary. If one company ever sees another company's canary, that is a leak, and the tests catch it.

On top of that app sit five AI features. Each was built the same way: list what could go wrong, write attacks that try it, then build the feature until the attacks fail.

Data model stages 1–2

Describe a new record type in plain English; the AI drafts it. Every draft is checked, and nothing changes until you approve it.

Ask documents stage 3

Ask questions about your company's policies. Answers only ever use your company's documents, and cite them.

Incident stage 5

Break something on purpose, then watch an AI investigate, explain the cause and suggest a fix that only you can approve.

Security lab

Run every attack against the real code, and against a deliberately unsafe version, side by side.

Evals stage 4

The automatic report card that runs on every change and blocks anything that makes results worse.

  1. Log in as fjord and open Ask documents. Ask about the expense limit.
  2. Switch to cinder and ask the same question. Different company, different answer.
  3. In Incident, start a queue backlog with the malicious instruction ticked, and run the Fooled AI. Watch its injected moves bounce off.
  4. In the Security lab, run a suite and compare the secure and unsafe columns.

Everything here runs on this computer with synthetic data. Without an API key, the AI is a recorded or scripted stand-in; with one, it is Claude.

What you are looking at

Your company's record types. Platform objects are shared by every company (each can add, hide or relabel fields for itself); custom objects belong to your company only. The AI can draft changes, but its reply is treated as untrusted: it must match a strict format, may only refer to objects your company can see, and is sent back with the errors (up to 3 tries) if it fails. Nothing changes until you approve.
